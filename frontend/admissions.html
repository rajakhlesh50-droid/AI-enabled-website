Financial Support

Scholarships & Fee Concessions

Merit should never be limited by financial boundaries. We award over ₹2.5 Crore in scholarships annually.

Chancellor's Merit Scholarship

Students scoring 90%+ in 10+2 receive up to 100% tuition waiver; 80%-89% get 50% tuition waiver; 70%-79% get 25% waiver.

Girl Child Empowerment

Special 15% tuition concession for female scholars pursuing B.Tech Engineering, Agriculture, and BBA programs to promote gender diversity in STEM.

Sports & Armed Forces Quota

20% fee waiver for national/state level sports medalists and wards of defense personnel, CRPF, and police martyrs.
