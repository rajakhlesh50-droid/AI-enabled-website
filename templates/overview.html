About CUTM

Shaping Lives, Empowering Communities

Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM) is a premier NAAC 'A+' accredited university committed to skill-integrated, practice-oriented higher education.

Who we are

A Center of Innovation & Social Impact

CUTM was founded with a profound commitment to building scalable, industry-aligned learning ecosystems. With multi-campus facilities and partnerships with leading industries, CUTM fosters technical competency, entrepreneurship, and sustainable community growth.

◎

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized, skill-integrated university that empowers youth, transforms livelihoods, and advances sustainable technologies.

↗

Our Mission

To provide experiential learning, foster research in emerging technologies and agriculture, and ensure holistic placement readiness.

✦

Our Approach

Skill courses embedded within degree programs, hands-on industrial live labs, 500+ bed hospital rotations, and university research farmlands.

Advanced Tech & Engineering Labs

Equipped with GPU computing, robotics cells, CAD/CAM machinery, and IoT testbeds.

Agricultural Research Ecosystem

Polyhouses, soil science labs, and precision farming plots for B.Sc. Agriculture students.

Healthcare & Hospital Clinicals

Multi-specialty hospital rotations and nursing labs for practical patient care training.
